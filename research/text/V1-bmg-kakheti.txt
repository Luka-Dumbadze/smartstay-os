Title: Staff Shortages and Seasonality Challenge Kakheti Hotels
Published: 2026-09-07 11:00:00

[paragraph 1] Staff shortages remain one of the main challenges for Georgia’s hotel sector in Kakheti, according to Amirbari Hospitality Agency Managing Partner Ketevan Amirbari. She said finding and retaining employees is particularly difficult in the region, with many workers moving to Tbilisi or abroad.

[paragraph 2] Seasonality further complicates the problem, as hotels often have to reduce staff after the peak season. This creates an unstable job market and encourages employees to seek more secure opportunities elsewhere, Amirbari said.

[paragraph 3] High staff turnover also affects service quality, making it harder for hotels to build a strong corporate culture and employee loyalty. Despite these challenges, demand for Kakheti hotels remains high, particularly for events and international gatherings, while the harvest season also brings increased activity.

[paragraph 4] Amirbari said management and workplace culture play a crucial role in a hotel’s success. Boutique, conceptual and high-end hotels are making efforts to retain their management and staff, helping them maintain service standards despite the region’s labor and seasonal challenges.
