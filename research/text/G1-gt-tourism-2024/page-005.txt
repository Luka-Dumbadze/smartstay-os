                                                                                                                                           January 31, 2025




2025 outlook
We expect 5.3mn tourists to visit Georgia in 2025 in our baseline
scenario, up from 5.1mn in 2024, assuming political instability
affects only 1Q25, with conditions normalizing thereafter. In a
pessimistic scenario, we do not expect a sharp decline in tourist
arrivals, as tourists from the Middle East and Asian countries are
expected to continue growing, having already shown growth during
the turbulent year of 2024. Additionally, possible reopening of the
Azeri land border is upside for tourism. We project tourism
revenues at $4.5bn in 2025, up from $4.4bn in 2024.
