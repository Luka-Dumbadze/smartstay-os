                                         Research                                                                      Hotel Price Index In Georgia

                                                                                      August 2026
• In August 2026, hotel price index in Georgia increased by 2.3% MoM, with the largest increase observed in Shida Kartli,
Kvemo Kartli, and Samtskhe-Javakheti compared to previous month.
• In August 2026, hotel price index in Georgia increased by 6.9% YoY, with the largest increase in Imereti, Racha and
Samegrelo-Zemo Svaneti.
• The average price of a room ranged from 134 GEL to 598 GEL in August 2026.

                                                                          Hotel Price Index
Month-over-month (MoM), the hotel price index3 increased by 2.4% for 3-, 4-, and 5-star hotels, and increased by 2.3% for
guesthouses. As a result, the hotel price index increased by 2.3% in August 2026 compared to July 2026.

Year-over-year (YoY), the hotel price index increased by 6.8% for 3-, 4-, and 5-star hotels, and increased by 7.2% for guesthouses.
As a result, the hotel price index increased by 6.9% in August 2026 compared to August 2025.
