July 2026 continued                     recovery            in non -residents’ non -cash spending,                                     while regional performance
remains uneven .


Overall transaction growth slightly outpaced spending growth, suggesting that activity remained strong while
average spending per transaction softened marginally . Adjara recorded the highest average transaction value in
both categories, followed by Kakheti and Tbilisi .

Tbilisi’s July performance remained positive across both categories . Total spending and average transaction
values increased in both hotels and restaurants, with particularly strong growth in restaurant spending .

Adjara’s July performance was hotel -led, with increases in both total hotel spending and average transaction
values . Restaurant spending posted only a modest annual increase, while average transaction values fell below
last year’s level, indicating an increase in the number of transactions .

In Kakheti , both total non - cash spending                                     and average transaction values                             increased             across         hotels               and
restaurants, with the growth more pronounced                                    in the restaurants segment .

Looking ahead, peak -season activity in August is expected                                              to support strong hotel performance                                 in Adjara and
continue d growth in Tbilisi and Kakheti .
