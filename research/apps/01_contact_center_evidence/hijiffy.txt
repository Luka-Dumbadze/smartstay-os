Answers to the Most Frequent Questions About HiJiffy
What is a hotel AI chatbot and how does it work?
A
hotel AI chatbot
is a conversational assistant that answers guest questions and handles bookings automatically, 24/7, across channels like a hotel website, WhatsApp, and social media. HiJiffy’s chatbot runs on Aplysia, an AI model built specifically for hospitality, which draws answers from your verified property knowledge and hands complex requests to your team with full context.
How much of guest communication can hotel AI actually automate?
HiJiffy automates up to 90% of routine guest communication – FAQs, booking questions, check-in and check-out, upselling, and reviews – across all channels. Hotels including
Kabannas
,
Macdonald Hotels & Resorts
, and
Hotel Gran Bilbao
run automation rates of 90% and above. The remaining conversations are routed to staff, pre-prioritised by urgency, so nothing high-stakes is missed.
How accurate is HiJiffy's AI, and can it give guests wrong information?
HiJiffy is built to prevent wrong answers. Its
Aplysia model
uses anti-hallucination guardrails that stop the AI from answering beyond your verified knowledge base or brand guidelines – so it will not invent a cancellation policy or an amenity you don’t offer. HiJiffy gives you per-source visibility into every answer the AI provides, directly inside the Inbox, so you can see exactly where each response came from. Reports resurface partially answered questions and knowledge gaps, so you can improve coverage over time. You always know what your AI is doing and why.
Can I trust the same AI across multiple properties without it going off-brand?
Yes. HiJiffy is built for
multi-property hotel groups
. Knowledge updates made at company level propagate automatically to every property, while the AI narrows context to each property’s specific details and brand nuances. This keeps answers consistent and on-brand across your entire portfolio, and turns every conversation into a cross-sell opportunity across the group.
What happens when the AI can't answer a guest's question?
When a question falls outside its verified knowledge, HiJiffy’s AI does not guess. It escalates the conversation to the right team automatically, passing along full context and conversation history, and flags urgency using real-time sentiment and intent detection. Guests always reach a human when they need one, and staff arrive prepared.
Is guest data handled in compliance with GDPR and security standards?
Yes. All communication through HiJiffy is encrypted and fully GDPR-compliant, and hotels retain full ownership and control of their guest data. Aplysia speaks only from verified information, safety systems check every reply for accuracy and brand alignment, and full technical and security details are published at
hijiffy.com/security-and-compliance.
Which channels does HiJiffy support?
HiJiffy centralises guest communication across webchat, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram, email, voice, and OTA native messaging, including Booking.com, Expedia and Airbnb – all in one Console. Its Booking.com and Expedia integrations are native and fully automated, covering the guest journey from reservation to 66 days post-stay with no manual steps from your team.
Which systems and PMS does HiJiffy integrate with?
HiJiffy integrates with 100+ hotel systems, including leading property management systems (PMS), booking engines, CRMs, and other hospitality tools, so guest data and messaging stay connected. PMS integration lets the AI use real context – arrival dates, room type, stay status – to give guests accurate, personalised answers and trigger automated campaigns.
Can HiJiffy help drive direct bookings and reduce OTA dependency?
Yes – it’s a core reason hotels choose HiJiffy. The AI booking assistant guides website visitors to book direct, webchat campaigns convert lookers before they leave for an OTA, and OTA native messaging brings guests who booked via Booking.com into a direct WhatsApp relationship for future stays.

Can HiJiffy send proactive campaigns, not just answer questions?
Yes. HiJiffy runs proactive webchat and WhatsApp campaigns across the full guest journey – welcome messages, pre-arrival, in-stay upselling, satisfaction surveys, and post-stay offers. Campaigns run on pre-built hospitality flows set up once, with no marketing expertise required, and achieve open rates above 80%, compared with roughly 20% for email.
How is HiJiffy different from other hotel chatbots and guest-experience suites?
HiJiffy is purpose-built around reliable AI automation and proactive communication, not a chatbot bolted onto a broader suite. Compared with basic chatbots, it adds anti-hallucination guardrails, per-source reporting, and multi-property governance. Compared with guest-experience suites where messaging is one feature among many, HiJiffy goes deeper on AI automation, full-channel coverage, and campaigns that drive direct revenue.
