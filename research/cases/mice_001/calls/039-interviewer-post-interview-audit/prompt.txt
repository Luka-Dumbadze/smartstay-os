What responsibility, if any, do you have for corporate or group enquiries about future stays and events?

### Respondent

Yes, you can take notes for this rehearsal. Please keep names and client details out of anything you share.

I’m the commercial director and general manager, so I’m responsible for whether a corporate enquiry becomes an offer we can deliver at acceptable terms. We have 24 rooms, so a request for 18 rooms affects most of the hotel.

Our sales manager owns the client relationship and coordinates the response. She can quote within approved rates and packages. I get involved when there’s a room discount, a question about holding inventory, or a trade-off with other business we might expect on those dates.

F&B and the chef confirm the menu, staffing and service arrangements alongside existing commitments. I can’t simply promise a private dinner or a particular service time without their input. The owner also retains authority over certain payment and cancellation exceptions.

So I don’t write every proposal, but when an enquiry needs several decisions, I’m responsible for getting those decisions together and giving Sales a clear answer they can stand behind.

## Turn 02 — Last difficult episode — simulated minutes 5–8

### Interviewer

What was the most recent corporate enquiry that was difficult to handle, if any? If none comes to mind, we can walk through the most recent enquiry.

### Respondent

The one that comes to mind arrived on 15 September, for a two-night retreat in November: 18 rooms for 36 people, a meeting room, two coffee breaks and a wine gala dinner.

The difficulty was getting a package close to their USD 10,000 budget that we could actually deliver. They initially wanted 18 twins, a private dinner with five premium wines, and a 10% deposit with the balance after the event. We could offer 14 twins and four doubles. Sales worked through alternatives with them: three wines, two tasting groups and a later dinner in a screened restaurant section. Meanwhile, I was reviewing the room discount and possible displacement, and the owner needed to decide on payment terms.

They cited the wait, but also preferred the other venue’s private dinner room and payment schedule. I regard it as a missed opportunity; I can’t say an earlier answer would definitely have won it.
