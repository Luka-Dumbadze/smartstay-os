### Respondent

I’m the general manager. We have 18 rooms, so on a normal shift I move between checking arrivals with reception, agreeing room priorities with our housekeeping lead, and dealing with anything staff cannot settle themselves. When that connection gets missed, it is my responsibility too. The newer staff need clear instructions and someone available to back them up.

### Respondent

Those times had started as requests, subject to housekeeping, but reception confirmed them without getting that confirmation.
