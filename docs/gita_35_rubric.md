### Official GITA Smartstay 3.0 Judging Rubric (Total: 35 Points)

1. Problem Scale & Understanding (Max 5 points):
- 4-5 pts: Problem is clearly defined; team explains its scale, importance, and urgency in detail. Demonstrates complete understanding of the challenge.
- 2-3 pts: Problem understanding is superficial; scale is not sufficiently justified or arguments are less convincing.
- 0-1 pts: Problem is not identified or description is unclear.

2. Solution (Max 5 points):
- 4-5 pts: Proposed solution is innovative, creative, and fully addresses the stated problem.
- 2-3 pts: Solution partially solves the problem or lacks novelty.
- 0-1 pts: Solution is inadequate or description is unclear.

3. Commercialization Potential (Max 5 points):
- 4-5 pts: Project has real potential to become commercially successful. Market, target audience, and revenue streams are clearly defined.
- 2-3 pts: Commercialization plan is vague or inadequate; market/audience analysis is superficial.
- 0-1 pts: Commercial future of the project is uncertain.

4. Team (Max 5 points):
- 4-5 pts: Team members are well-prepared, roles are clear, and they convincingly present the project.
- 2-3 pts: Presentation lacks consistency or lacks necessary skills.
- 0-1 pts: Team is unprepared or roles are undefined.

5. Prototype (Max 10 points - The Decisive Category!):
- 8-10 pts: Prototype is functional, well-prepared, and convincingly demonstrates the viability of the idea.
- 5-7 pts: Prototype partially works, showing core aspects of the idea.
- 2-4 pts: Prototype is faulty/buggy, but core concept can still be communicated.
- 0-1 pts: Prototype is not presented or completely non-functional.

6. Presentation Quality (Max 5 points):
- 4-5 pts: Presentation is clear, structured, and consistent. Visual materials (slides) enhance content and are easy to understand.
- 2-3 pts: Presentation lacks consistency or visuals need improvement.
- 0-1 pts: Presentation is poorly prepared or lacks structure.

### Key Success Factors Announced by GITA:
- Measurable results: Quantifiable savings, time reduction, or revenue lift in numbers.
- Realistic implementation cost: Low barrier to entry and clear payback period, even for small regional boutique hotels.
- Integration with existing systems: Interoperability with existing hotel PMS, Channel Managers, and POS (e.g. Exely, Cloudbeds, Poster, FINA).
- Readiness for pilot: Immediately testable in a real Georgian hotel, natively in Georgian language.
- Rule: Do not present an already existing/operating commercial project on the market (must be built for this hackathon).
