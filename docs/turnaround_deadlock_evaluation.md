**[I] Money, time, and exposure were mostly preserved correctly, with one important presentation risk.**

- **Cash:** The synthetic reconciliation is **420 − 60 = 360 GEL retained** (**Turn 07**). The allowance and refund entries describe one compensation event. They do not establish 120 GEL of loss. Retained payment is not profit or contribution margin.
- **Opportunity revenue:** The **USD 40** late-checkout quote was conditional, unaccepted, and never charged (**Turns 05 and 07**). It establishes neither collectible lost revenue nor a cash loss. No currency conversion or margin assumption is justified.
- **Guest time:** **77 minutes** elapsed from arrival to keys; **47 minutes** elapsed after the original 12:30 assurance (**Turns 06 and 08**). Neither is staff labor. The revised approximately 13:15 estimate was conditional; a 13:17 handover should not automatically become another counted failure.
- **Staff effort:** The upstairs visit was described, but its duration and any repeated work were not measured. Cleaning-start, inspection-start, call, and writing times are missing (**Turns 06 and 10**). `staff_rework_minutes: null` is appropriate.
- **Exposure:** The synthetic register contains **six arriving parties**, with **one documented affected party and five unknown promise histories** (**Turn 08**). This is not a fully ascertained one-in-six failure rate. Same-minute arrival and keys do not establish compliance with earlier promises.
- **Normalization:** The case’s `30` follows the stipulated arithmetic, `1 × 30 / 1`. It is only a normalization of a documented minimum from one four-hour window. Presenting it as “30 incidents per month” would be misleading. It supplies no recurrence evidence.

**[I] Capacity and information problems must remain separate causal claims.**

Within the synthetic account, reception lacked dependable progress information and gave an assurance before checking. However, there is no historical state record proving that a displayed status was stale or incorrect. A room marked dirty can be accurately marked dirty while providing insufficient detail about progress. Missing read timestamps also do not establish an ignored or failed message.

The physical constraint is substantial: 103 had not started at the remembered check; the two cleaners worked together; suitable alternative rooms were reported occupied; and the guests declined a double (**Turns 06 and 11**). Those facts explain why information alone could not produce a ready twin at noon.

For 201, the same team had the room released **eight recorded minutes before arrival** (**Turn 11**). Its successful outcome combines clearer confirmation with more time before arrival. Neither the benefit of confirmation nor a physical-capacity benefit was isolated.
